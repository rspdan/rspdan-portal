## Leave something another person can use

The story, school book, source browser and retained study have different forms. A reader can move between them for a purpose without making them interchangeable. An explanation can lead back to its source. A source can prompt a different reading. A later correction can change what someone carries into the next attempt.

The March literary body also leaves room for creative modes, rest and an unfinished movement shaped by lived experience. The next contribution might be a passage, a visual relationship or something played on the Instrument. Its value will include what it lets another person understand, make or share. That consequence remains open to the work and to Dan’s direction.

Begin with the episode or a visual relationship that interests you. Follow one passage back to its source, notice what changes for you or remains unclear, and bring that reading back to the conversation with the passage that prompted it. Your response can give the next person another way through.
