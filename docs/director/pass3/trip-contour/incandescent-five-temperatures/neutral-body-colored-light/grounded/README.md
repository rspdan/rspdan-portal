# Grounded incandescent temperature comparison

The current contact treatment is combined with the five-temperature experiment. Trip remains balanced white at 6,500 K inside the black outline; exterior glow and projected light vary from 10,000 K to 2,700 K. The approved incandescent intensity (`tripGlow=1`), pose, camera, lighting and black outline are preserved. Head flare is off.

The intentional changes from the [preceding experiment](../README.md) are a floor height of zero instead of −0.005 and local attenuation of exterior glow at the posed soles' contact with the actual supporting surface. No root position or pose is snapped. Dan approved the [contact-on appearance](../../../ground-contact/local-mask/contact-on.png); this sheet preserves it across the five temperatures.

The optional body-temperature control remains separate from emitted-light temperature. The earlier [linked body-and-light option](../../body-temperature/README.md) is retained. No speculative plastic texture is added.

[Receiving](receiving.json) records 15 passing state and pixel checks, five actual browser renders, exact take states, image hashes and pinned source hashes. [Pixel receiving](pixel-receiving.json) confirms white body samples stay identical across temperatures, exterior halo and receivers change color, and neutral body/head-halo samples match the preceding balanced frame. Each frame records two actual sole contacts. The sheet is a browser screenshot of the HTML gallery displaying unmodified render images.

The [ground-contact receiving](../../../ground-contact/README.md) separately covers lifted feet, hidden/raised floors, Stage platforms and low camera angles. This is a local support-surface approximation, not a collision solver or complete volumetric light simulation. Physical ACHE performance is not established by browser emulation.
