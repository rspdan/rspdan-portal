# Director: pose, lighting and stage

This update gives the Director connected hand and foot posing, editable lighting and set pieces, custom backdrops, and independent outline, glow, head-flare and hand-light controls. All three modes share manipulation tools and the session's 60-step Undo/Redo history. Existing saved takes remain readable.

## Directing

- **Pose:** select a hand or foot and hold/drag, or use Move. The two connected limb segments bend toward that endpoint and stop at their reachable limit. Hips movement/rotation keeps reachable feet planted during the gesture. Whole character moves the rig together. Head, torso, shoulders, elbows and knees retain rotation controls. Enable **Stretch** for deliberate freeform joint displacement/scaling.
- **Lighting:** add a point, spot or directional light, set color/intensity, move it and aim it with the shared gizmos. Attach a light to either hand tip or the head; its editable offset follows the rig through posing and playback. One-click head and hand lights start with a visible ball. Choose ball, beam or light only, with separate size and effect strength. Ambient fill remains adjustable. Frame includes light controls; return to Pose and Frame for a close character view.
- **Stage:** choose a color or local raster backdrop, adjust its fit and the floor, then add, transform or remove boxes, spheres and panels. The background image is embedded in the saved take and renders inside the canvas.
- **Character appearance:** both characters have outline enable, black-to-white color and weight. Trip has a white base with retained shading. Glow controls light spilling beyond the surface and onto nearby sets. Body and emitted-light temperatures can be linked, or the body can stay at balanced white while only the exterior glow and projected light change temperature. The black contour remains visible beneath the outward bleed. Low LED stays close to the shaded base, while incandescent intensity expands the spill. Head flare is a separate control, initially Off. Head and hand sources also have individual brightness, color and optical controls.
- **Motion:** key the authored poses, choose hold/linear/easing, length, FPS and output size. An unkeyed pose displaced by scrub or playback is recoverable through Undo. Appearance, lighting and stage are global take settings in this version; attached lights follow the animated bones, but effect intensity has no separate animation track.

Head and individual light sources offer Compact glow, Glow + streak, Starburst and Multi-Iris starting looks. Expand Lens components to mix glow, rays, streak and iris reflections, or adjust spread, rotation and ray count. Preset selection resets those parts to the selected starting look. Light intensity and optical intensity remain separate, and camera movement/occlusion changes the optical response without automatic time flicker. Dan identifies Glow and Streak as his main tools for effects and focus, with Multi-Iris combined with other effects for Lighthouse beams and Trip's Usic powers.

New stages place the floor at the character's authored ground level. A small contact mask follows each posed sole and its actual upward-facing support surface, suppressing the exterior halo at contact while preserving side glow and projected room light. It fades as the foot lifts and also works on near-horizontal Stage platforms. Existing takes retain their explicitly saved floor heights. Dan accepted the corrected warm-glow and close-up images as grounded while retaining the sense of a comic emerging into3D.

The smooth browser ball remains a prototype rather than a reproduction of the staticky ball in his forest drawing.
